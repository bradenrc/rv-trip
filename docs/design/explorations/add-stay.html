Resolutions I'd file (★) — react in chat
  • ★ Search anchor: trip destination → first located stop → ask; IP fallback removed. When adding from a specific stop, that stop is the anchor. Chip always visible + overridable ("near ✕ / search near…").
  • ★ Add stay = lodging reservation on the stop, check-in/out defaulting to the stop's span ("covers your whole stay"), nights stepper for partial stays; splitting a stop's lodging (2 nights here, 1 there) creates a second stay, not a drag puzzle.
  • ★ Ideas keep the maybe lane — a "considering" hotel stays an idea; the stay flow has a "just considering?" escape down to the shelf, and promoting an idea to a stay inherits the span default.
  • Open for you: should "Add stay" also create the stop when the trip has none yet (fresh Bellingham trip → hotel first, stop inferred from it)? My lean: yes — the hotel IS the anchor on a Fly & Stay.
